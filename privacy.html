Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Souvion AI, a Discord bot, may process information when people interact with it. Please review this policy and ensure it matches the bot's actual code and configuration before publishing.

1. Information That May Be Processed

  • Discord information: Discord user IDs, server IDs, and message content needed to process commands, bot mentions, and requests.
  • Saved knowledge: Information intentionally submitted through knowledge-saving features, which may include global or server-specific knowledge.
  • Configuration: Server-specific settings, such as a custom prefix, if that feature is used.
  • Operational information: Error details and technical logs generated to operate, debug, and protect the bot.

The exact information processed depends on the features enabled and how the bot is used. Do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, payment details, or other sensitive information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to answer user requests, provide bot features, retrieve saved knowledge, maintain configuration, troubleshoot errors, protect the service, and comply with legal obligations.

3. AI Providers

When AI responses are requested, relevant prompts and context may be sent to configured AI providers, which may include Groq, Google Gemini, and OpenRouter. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not send confidential or sensitive information to the bot.

4. Hosting and Storage

The bot may run on Railway. If GitHub-backed knowledge storage is enabled, saved knowledge may be stored in a GitHub repository. Access, retention, and security depend on the actual configuration and the policies of those providers.

5. Sharing

Information may be processed by the service providers needed to operate the bot, when you request a feature that uses them, or when disclosure is required by law. We do not intend to sell personal information.

6. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the stated purposes or as required by law. Saved knowledge and configuration may remain until updated or deleted. Some technical logs or provider records may have separate retention periods.
